# 3.2.S.4.4 Batch Analyses (excerpt): Drug Substance Batch TLC-2512

**Active substance:** Tolucimab (CBP-118)
**Document number:** CBP118-S444-ADD-07 | **Date:** 19 January 2026

Batch TLC-2512 was manufactured at commercial scale (12,000 L) at Speke in November 2025 and released on 16 January 2026 against specification CBP118-SPEC-DS version 5.0. Testing used working reference standard WRS-03.
