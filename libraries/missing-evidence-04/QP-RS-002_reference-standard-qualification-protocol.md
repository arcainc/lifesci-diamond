# Protocol: Qualification of Tolucimab Working Reference Standards

**Protocol number:** QP-RS-002 | **Version:** 2.0 | **Approved:** 5 January 2022
**Applies to:** WRS-03 and subsequent working reference standards
**Owner:** QC Biologics, Corbridge Biopharma Manufacturing, Speke, Liverpool

## 1. Source material

A commercial drug substance batch that (a) met all release specifications, (b) is representative of the current process, and (c) has no open deviations affecting quality. The batch is aliquoted into 0.5 mL cryovials under controlled conditions and frozen at ≤ -65 °C.

## 2. Testing

Testing is performed on at least three vials selected from the beginning, middle and end of the fill. PRS-01 is tested in the same run as the candidate standard.

| Test                                                    | Acceptance criterion                                                      |
| ------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Protein concentration by A280 (triplicate, three vials) | Concentration assigned from the mean; RSD ≤ 2.0%                          |
| Peptide map                                             | Profile comparable to PRS-01; no new or missing peaks                     |
| SE-HPLC monomer                                         | Within ± 1.0% absolute of PRS-01                                          |
| icIEF main peak                                         | Within ± 3.0% absolute of PRS-01                                          |
| CE-SDS reduced (HC + LC)                                | Within ± 2.0% absolute of PRS-01                                          |
| N-glycan profile                                        | Each major species (G0F, G1F, G2F, Man5) within ± 3.0% absolute of PRS-01 |
| Relative potency (cell-based, six independent assays)   | Mean 90% to 110% of PRS-01; 95% confidence interval within 85% to 115%    |
| Fill homogeneity                                        | Concentration of beginning, middle and end vials within ± 3% of the mean  |

## 3. Assignment and release

If all criteria are met, the candidate is assigned a potency of 100% (or the measured mean if outside 95% to 105%, with QA justification) and released by QA with a certificate of analysis. The initial re-test period is 24 months, extendable on the basis of stability data.

## 4. Documentation

Results are documented in a qualification report (QR-WRS-nn) and a certificate of analysis (CoA-WRS-nn). Any failure is investigated; a failed candidate may not be used.
