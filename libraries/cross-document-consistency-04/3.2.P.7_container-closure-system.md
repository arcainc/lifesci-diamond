# 3.2.P.7 Container Closure System

**Product:** Calvorastat for injection, 100 mg/vial
**Applicant:** Brookline Therapeutics, Inc. | **NDA:** 229604
**Document number:** BRK-CLV-P7 | **Version:** 2.0 | **Date:** 30 June 2026

## 1. Description

The primary container closure system consists of:

| Component | Description                                                                                                                                                   | Supplier            | Quality standard          |
| --------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------- | ------------------------- |
| Vial      | 10R clear Type I borosilicate glass tubing vial, nominal capacity 10 mL, 20 mm blow-back finish                                                               | Vitralux GmbH       | USP <660>, Ph. Eur. 3.2.1 |
| Stopper   | 20 mm two-leg lyophilization stopper, bromobutyl rubber (formulation BB-4410), fluoropolymer film on the product-contact surface, supplied ready-to-sterilize | Elastora Inc.       | USP <381>, Ph. Eur. 3.2.9 |
| Seal      | 20 mm aluminum seal with polypropylene flip-off button                                                                                                        | Sealtech Components | Drawing SC-20-FO          |

The stopper is not manufactured with natural rubber latex. Secondary packaging is a folding carton holding one vial; the carton is not needed for light protection (3.2.P.8.1) but is required for traceability of the room-temperature storage date.

## 2. Suitability

Suitability of the bromobutyl stopper for this product was demonstrated by:

- extractables studies (3.2.P.2.4) and leachables testing of stability samples at 36 months, with no leachable above the analytical evaluation threshold;
- container closure integrity by headspace oxygen analysis (validated, sensitivity equivalent to 0.5 µm leak) at release and on stability;
- residual moisture of the lyophilized cake maintained at ≤ 1.5% throughout the stability program.

## 3. Change history

The container closure system is unchanged since the pivotal clinical batches. No alternative stopper is registered.
