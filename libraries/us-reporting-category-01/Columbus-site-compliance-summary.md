## 2. Operations currently performed

Manufacture, primary packaging (HDPE bottles and blisters) and testing of immediate-release film-coated tablets for four approved applications (two NDAs and two ANDAs held by Kestrel), and of immediate-release hard capsules for one ANDA. Wet granulation, fluid-bed drying, compression and aqueous film coating are all in routine commercial use at the site.

The two 2025 observations concerned (1) incomplete equipment cleaning log entries for one granulation suite and (2) environmental monitoring trend reports not reviewed within the SOP timeframe. Responses were submitted on 27 February 2025; corrective actions were verified closed by site QA on 30 June 2025. FDA has not requested further action.
