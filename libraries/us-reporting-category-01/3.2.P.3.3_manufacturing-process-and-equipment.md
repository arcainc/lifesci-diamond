# 3.2.P.3.3 Description of Manufacturing Process and Process Controls

**Product:** SERDOVA (serdoxetine hydrochloride) tablets, 25 mg and 50 mg | **NDA:** 229481
**Site:** Kestrel Pharmaceuticals, Raleigh Plant
**Version:** 3.0 | **Date:** 15 January 2025

## 1. Batch formula and size

Immediate-release, film-coated tablets from a common granulation (dose-proportional). Approved commercial batch size: 62.5 kg of final blend, equivalent to 250,000 tablets (50 mg, 250 mg core) or 500,000 tablets (25 mg, 125 mg core).

## 2. Process description

1. Dispensing of serdoxetine hydrochloride, lactose monohydrate, microcrystalline cellulose, and croscarmellose sodium (intragranular).
2. Dry mixing and wet granulation with purified water in a high-shear granulator.
3. Wet milling, then drying in a fluid-bed dryer to LOD 1.0% to 2.5%.
4. Dry milling through a conical mill (1.0 mm screen).
5. Blending with extragranular croscarmellose sodium and microcrystalline cellulose in a bin blender, then lubrication with magnesium stearate.
6. Compression on a rotary tablet press.
7. Film coating with an aqueous PVA-based coating suspension in a perforated pan coater to a weight gain of 3.0% to 4.0%.
8. Packaging into 30-count and 90-count HDPE bottles with an induction-sealed polypropylene child-resistant closure and a 1 g silica gel desiccant canister.

## 4. In-process controls

| Step        | Control                                    | Limit                                |
| ----------- | ------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------ |
| Drying      | Loss on drying                             | 1.0% to 2.5%                         |
| Blending    | Blend uniformity (validation batches only) | Per validation protocol              |
| Compression | Individual tablet weight (every 15 min)    | Target ± 5%                          |
| Compression | Hardness (every 30 min)                    | 50 mg: 8 to 14 kP; 25 mg: 5 to 10 kP |
| Compression | Friability (start, middle, end)            | NMT 0.8%                             |
| Coating     | Weight gain (end of each pan load)         | 3.0% to 4.0%                         |

## 5. Process validation

Three consecutive commercial batches per strength were validated at Raleigh in 2021 (3.2.P.3.5).
