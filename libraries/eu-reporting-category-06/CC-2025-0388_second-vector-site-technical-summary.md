# Technical Summary for Change Control CC-2025-0388: Second Manufacturing Site for LV-LUM-19

**Product:** Lumacel | **Date raised:** 30 June 2025 | **Prepared by:** Vector Supply and Technical Operations
**Version:** 1.1, 22 September 2025

## 1. Proposal

Add Solvane Biologics BV, Bioscience Park, Leiden, the Netherlands, as a second manufacturer of LV-LUM-19. Kestner Viral Vectors remains approved and continues to supply; Solvane is an additional source to secure supply from 2026.

## 2. Site status

| Item              | Detail                                                                                                                                                      |
| ----------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| GMP certificate   | Issued by the Dutch Health and Youth Care Inspectorate (IGJ) on 12 March 2024, scope includes manufacture of biological active substances and viral vectors |
| Experience        | Manufactures lentiviral and AAV vectors for four other clients (clinical and commercial)                                                                    |
| Quality agreement | Signed 5 May 2025                                                                                                                                           |

## 3. Technology transfer

The registered vector process description (3.2.S.2.3 section 3) and specification (section 4) are used unchanged. The same plasmid supplier and the same producer cell bank (PCB-293T-02; vials shipped from Kestner in April 2025) are used.

| Item           | Kestner (approved)                                  | Solvane (proposed)                                                     |
| -------------- | --------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Bioreactor     | 250 L single-use stirred tank, 200 L working volume | 500 L single-use stirred tank (different vendor), 200 L working volume |
| Anion exchange | Packed-bed column, skid vendor A                    | Membrane adsorber, skid vendor B                                       |
| TFF            | Hollow fibre, 750 kDa                               | Hollow fibre, 750 kDa, same supplier                                   |
| Fill           | Manual fill in grade A cabinet                      | Automated vial filler in isolator                                      |

Raw material supplier qualification at Solvane is in progress; a bill of materials comparison was not part of this summary.

Release testing of Solvane lots is performed by the approved laboratories (Kestner QC for all tests except replication-competent lentivirus, which stays at Veltris).
