**INTERNAL. Not for submission. Not reviewed by FDA. Official record is the FDA meeting minutes.**
**Author:** Head of CMC Regulatory, Lanark Genetic Medicines | **Circulated:** 12 May 2026, 17:40

Teleconference, 60 minutes. FDA attendees from the Office of Therapeutic Products (product reviewers, CMC lead, statistics reviewer) and the Office of Compliance and Biologics Quality.

## Q1 (analytical comparability, no clinical data)

- Discussion ran about 25 minutes. My impression: FDA did not object in principle to an analytical-only package for this change, but several questions on the capsid content method.
- FDA asked why full/empty is only by AUC and whether we have an orthogonal method (cryo-EM or CDMS). We said CDMS is in development.
- FDA asked about differences in residual host cell DNA fragment size and packaged host cell DNA between adherent and suspension processes. Said they would "provide further comment in the minutes".
- Nobody from FDA said "agree" in so many words. VP Process Development thinks it was a yes; I would call it "not a no".

- Written response to come in minutes.
